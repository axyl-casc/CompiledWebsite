Privacy Policy

Privacy guidelines, local data handling, and network usage policies for the Compiled Android application.

Application: Compiled (Android) Last Updated: September 3, 2026 Effective Date: September 3, 2026
PRIVACY-FIRST DESIGN: Compiled is designed as an offline-first board game. We do not require account registration, do not collect personal identification information, and do not track your real-world identity or physical location.

1. Overview & Application Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how Compiled ("the Application", "the App", "we", "our", or "us") treats data and user privacy. This policy applies specifically to the Compiled game application distributed on Google Play for Android devices, as well as this official website.

Compiled is a competitive, turn-based tabletop strategy game inspired by 68k-style assembly programming where players compete over a simulated shared CPU. We believe gaming software should respect player privacy by default.

2. Information Collection

We do not collect Personally Identifiable Information (PII).

When you install and play Compiled on your Android device:

  • No Account Required: You do not need to create an account, sign in, or provide your name, email address, phone number, or password.
  • No Contact or Address Book Access: The App never requests access to your device's contacts, call logs, or SMS.
  • No Location Tracking: The App does not request or access GPS, coarse location, or fine location data.
  • No Audio or Camera Access: The App does not request or use your microphone, camera, or sensors unrelated to basic screen orientation.
  • No Behavioral Tracking for Advertising: We do not track you across third-party apps or websites for targeted advertising.

3. Local Data Storage

To deliver a seamless offline gaming experience, Compiled stores limited game-state data locally on your Android device. This data is strictly stored within the App's isolated sandbox storage (such as Android SharedPreferences and internal application files) and is never transmitted to any external private server.

Types of data stored locally:

  • Game Preferences & Settings: User preferences such as sound effect volume toggles, display settings, and animation speed.
  • Tutorial Progress: Flags indicating completed tutorial modules and rules reference unlocks.
  • Single-Player Match State: Active board state and AI opponent difficulty settings for resuming interrupted offline single-player games.
  • Local Statistics & Match History: Win/loss counts, completed secret objectives, and local gameplay records.

This data belongs to you. Because it is stored locally on your device, clearing the app's cache/data via Android Settings or uninstalling the app permanently removes this information.

4. Internet & Network Usage

Compiled is built as an offline-first game:

  • Offline Core Gameplay: Core gameplay—including single-player against AI, pass-and-play local multiplayer, and tutorial modes—functions entirely without an active internet connection.
  • Network Connections: The App does not ping proprietary servers during standard gameplay. Network activity is limited to standard operating system services provided by the Android OS and Google Play (such as checking for application updates or license verification).
Future Online Multiplayer Note: If future releases introduce online networked multiplayer or cloud cross-save functionality, this policy will be updated before rollout, and players will be explicitly notified within the app with clear opt-in controls.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Compiled is suitable for general audiences and puzzle enthusiasts of all ages. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families policy:

  • We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in applicable European jurisdictions).
  • Because the app does not collect personal information from any user, no children's data is acquired, shared, or processed.

If a parent or guardian believes that personal data has inadvertently been provided, please contact us immediately so we can ensure proper handling.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Since we do not operate user accounts or external databases collecting your information, we retain zero personal data on external servers.

How to delete your data: You have complete control over all data stored by the App on your device:

  1. Open your Android device's Settings.
  2. Navigate to Apps > Compiled > Storage & Cache.
  3. Tap Clear Storage (or Clear Data) to erase all local save states and preferences.
  4. Alternatively, uninstalling Compiled immediately deletes all locally stored application data.

8. Security

The security of your device and data is paramount. Compiled leverages the built-in application sandboxing provided by the Android operating system. Local application files are inaccessible to other standard applications on your device. Furthermore, because the App does not transmit your game state across the internet, there is no risk of in-transit interception of personal data.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy to reflect modifications in game features (e.g., adding online multiplayer), regulatory standards, or Google Play policy updates.

When updates occur, we will revise the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document. We encourage players to periodically review this page. Significant changes will be announced in release notes on the Google Play Store listing.
