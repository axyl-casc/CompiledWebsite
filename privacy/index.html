Compiled Android app

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Compiled handles information in the Android app and on this website.

Last updated: September 22, 2026 App package: com.compiled.game Version: 1.0.10

Compiled is built around local gameplay. The current app does not include account sign-in, ads, analytics SDKs, or a cloud save service.

Information We Collect

Compiled does not require an account, email address, phone number, contacts, camera, microphone, or location. You can choose local player names when setting up pass and play.

The current app source does not include third-party advertising or analytics libraries. Gameplay, saved tutorial progress, settings, player names, rated profile statistics, hands, objectives, and match state are handled on your device.

Local Gameplay Data

The app saves your settings, completed lessons, custom match setup, pass-and-play roster, and rated profile on your device. Your rated profile includes your level and match record. An unfinished rated match is also saved so you can resume it after reopening the app.

During play, cards, objectives, the board, and CPU registers are kept on the device so the game can run. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app removes its local data.

The app does not send match state, private objectives, or player names to a Compiled server.

Network Use

The current app is designed for rated and custom singleplayer games against computer opponents, and same-device pass and play. Rated levels and profile statistics are stored locally.

If you download the app or an update through Google Play, Google may process information needed to provide the Play Store. Google handles that information under the Google Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

Compiled does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app does not require accounts or collect personal details in the current build, there is no child account data for us to sell, share, or use for advertising.

Future Features

If a future release adds cloud saves, analytics, crash reporting, account features, or another service that changes how data is handled, this policy should be updated before that release is distributed.

Contact

Questions about Compiled or this policy can be sent to compiledapp@gmail.com.
