Compiled Android app

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Compiled handles information in the Android app and on this website.

Last updated: September 8, 2026 App package: com.compiled.game Version: 1.0.2

Compiled is built around local gameplay. The current app does not include account sign-in, ads, analytics SDKs, or a cloud save service.

Information We Collect

Compiled does not ask you to create an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, camera, microphone, or location.

The current app source does not include third-party advertising or analytics libraries. Gameplay, tutorial progress during a session, settings, hands, objectives, and match state are handled on your device.

Local Gameplay Data

During play, the app keeps the current match, settings, player roster, cards, board state, and CPU registers on the device so the game can run. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app removes app data stored by Android for Compiled.

The app does not send match state, private objectives, or player names to a Compiled server.

Network Use

The current app is designed for singleplayer and same-device pass and play.

If you join the closed test or download an update through Google Play, Google may process information needed to provide the Play Store. Google handles that information under the Google Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

Compiled does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app does not require accounts or collect personal details in the current build, there is no child account data for us to sell, share, or use for advertising.

Future Features

If a future release adds cloud saves, analytics, crash reporting, account features, or another service that changes how data is handled, this policy should be updated before that release is distributed.

Contact

Questions about Compiled or this policy can be sent to compiledapp@gmail.com.
