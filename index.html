How to play

Place a card.
Change the game.

The cards are commands that change numbers or redirect play around a loop. A card you place can help you now and change what happens on someone else’s turn.

  1. 01

    Choose your move

    Pick one of the six cards in your hand. Place it in an empty space or replace a card already on the board.

  2. 02

    Press Run

    Your marker moves through the loop and follows its cards, carrying out up to five commands. You draw a replacement card for your hand.

  3. 03

    Reach your objective

    Meet both parts of your secret objective at the end of your run to win. Plan ahead as other players change the board between your turns.

Ways to play

Solo or around the table.

You + computer rivals

Singleplayer

Take on one to three computer opponents. Choose Easy, Normal, Hard, or Expert difficulty to find the challenge you want.

One device · 2–4 players

Pass and play

Take turns with friends on the same device, with computer opponents if you like. Handoff screens keep each player’s cards and objective hidden.

Make room for your strategy. Both modes offer small, medium, and large boards with 8, 12, or 16 spaces.

The cards

Start simple.
Add more possibilities.

Begin with a small set of commands, then try more ways to change values and steer the loop. Everyone in a match uses the same card set.

9 cards

Basic

Move or swap values, add and subtract, or place a card that stops a run. The starting set for new players.

19 cards

Intermediate

Expand the Basic set with multiplication, division, logic, and jumps that change the route through the board.

31 cards

Advanced

Play with the full deck, adding more number operations and new ways to shift and rearrange values.

Move up after the tutorial, or choose “Try Anyway” in Settings when you want to explore.
